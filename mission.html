LEGACY HONOR 911
Mission Amendment

A record intended to grow with time.

Legacy Honor 911 serves as an independent index and research gateway for publicly accessible information concerning people associated with the World Trade Center on September 11, 2001. The project provides a respectful starting point for discovering professional identities, careers, organizations, accomplishments, and other documented context, while directing visitors toward further research in the public record.

Long-term stewardship

The project is intended to develop over time. Future research, writing, verification, preservation, hosting, and related work may expand the index and improve its usefulness. The pace of that work will depend on available resources and, above all, on the quality and reliability of the historical record available for each person.

Where appropriate, an individual's name or profile may provide a pathway to external search, reference, archival, or other publicly accessible resources. The purpose is to help visitors continue their own research rather than to suggest that Legacy Honor 911 possesses exclusive or undisclosed information.

What Legacy Honor 911 does — and does not — claim

Legacy Honor 911 does not claim to be an official memorial registry, authoritative biography, family archive, employer record, government database, or exclusive source of information about the people it honors. It is an independent index and gateway intended to make responsible discovery easier.

The project does not claim exclusive knowledge of the people it honors, and it does not present private or unknown information as a benefit of visiting the site. Inclusion is based on the project's research criteria and available public documentation, and visitors are encouraged to consult the underlying public record for themselves.

Open access to the record

The goal is not to create a paywall around remembrance. The site's educational and indexing material is intended to remain publicly accessible. If the project receives resources that allow additional research or preservation work, those resources are intended to support the ongoing stewardship and expansion of the index—not to sell access to a person's story.

Independence and accuracy: Legacy Honor 911 is an independent project. Inclusion does not imply endorsement, sponsorship, authorization, or representation by a family, estate, employer, organization, government agency, memorial institution, or other third party. Information should be supported by reliable documentation, and corrections are welcomed when credible evidence is provided.
