LEGACY HONOR 911
Mission Amendment

A record intended to grow with time.

Legacy Honor 911 is committed to building an enduring, independent educational and memorial record of the professional lives, careers, organizations, accomplishments, and aspirations associated with the World Trade Center on September 11, 2001. As the project grows, its aim is to broaden that record responsibly and, wherever reliable documentation permits, continue expanding coverage so that more of the people lost are remembered as the individuals and professionals they were.

Long-term stewardship

The project is intended to develop over time. Future research, writing, verification, preservation, hosting, and related work may expand the site's scope and depth. The pace of that work will depend on available resources and, above all, on the quality and reliability of the historical record available for each person.

Any resources used to sustain or expand the project do not change its purpose. Legacy Honor 911 will continue to distinguish historical documentation from financial activity and will not present a digital asset, personal income, or other funding source as a payment for access to a person's story.

Open access to the record

The goal is not to create a paywall around remembrance. The site's educational material is intended to remain publicly accessible, while the work of researching and expanding it is treated as an ongoing responsibility.

Independence and accuracy: Legacy Honor 911 is an independent project. Inclusion does not imply endorsement, sponsorship, authorization, or representation by a family, estate, employer, organization, government agency, memorial institution, or other third party. Information should be supported by reliable documentation, and corrections are welcomed when credible evidence is provided.
