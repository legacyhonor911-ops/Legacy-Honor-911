Purpose

Our Mission

Legacy Honor 911 exists to remember and preserve the professional lives of people associated with the World Trade Center on September 11, 2001.

The project focuses on the work people built during their lives: their careers, leadership, entrepreneurship, organizations, ambitions, professional achievements, and contributions to their fields.

The goal is not to reduce anyone to the circumstances of September 11. It is to preserve evidence that these were people who built careers, led organizations, served clients, developed businesses, pursued ambitions, and contributed to professional communities.

Legacy Honor 911 is intended to remain an independent educational and memorial resource.

Core Principles

  • Remember the individual.
  • Preserve professional history.
  • Respect families and estates.
  • Distinguish documented fact from interpretation.
  • Encourage independent research.
  • Maintain transparency about independence.
About

Independent by Design

Legacy Honor 911 is an independent educational and memorial project created to research and preserve publicly available information about professional lives associated with the World Trade Center on September 11, 2001.

The project is not intended to replace established memorials, historical archives, government records, family histories, or institutional collections. Instead, it is intended to provide another accessible place where professional histories can be remembered and researched.

Important distinction: Legacy Honor 911 is independent and does not claim to represent, speak for, or act on behalf of any featured individual, family, estate, survivor, employer, institution, government agency, or memorial organization unless that relationship is expressly established and identified.
