The Purpose

Why Professional Legacy?

History is often remembered through dates, photographs, buildings, and headlines. Legacy Honor 911 takes another approach: remembering people through the professional work they built, the organizations they served, and the expertise they brought to their fields.

This project is intended as an educational and memorial resource — preserving a small portion of the professional history surrounding September 11, 2001.

The goal is not to rank lives or suggest that one person's life mattered more than another's. It is to preserve professional legacies that might otherwise become little more than names in historical records.
