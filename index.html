Our Mission

Legacy Honor 911 is an independent educational and memorial project created to preserve a record of professional achievement and business legacy connected to September 11, 2001.

The project focuses on accomplishments, careers, leadership, entrepreneurship, and the organizations these individuals helped build or lead.

The purpose is not to rank lives or compare the value of one person against another. Each person represented here deserves individual remembrance and respect.

We seek to encourage historical research, responsible remembrance, and continued learning about the people whose professional lives were interrupted on that day.
