Those We Honor

Legacy Honor 911 is dedicated to preserving the professional stories, accomplishments, leadership, ambitions, and enduring legacies of people whose lives were connected to the World Trade Center on September 11, 2001.

01

Rick Rescorla

Security Leadership • Morgan Stanley

Rick Rescorla was a decorated Vietnam veteran and a senior security professional at Morgan Stanley. He became widely known for his leadership, preparation, and insistence on evacuation procedures that helped save thousands of lives on September 11, 2001.

John P. O'Neill

Counterterrorism Leadership • Port Authority

John O'Neill was a highly respected FBI counterterrorism expert who later became the Director of Security for the World Trade Center. His career was defined by investigations, intelligence, leadership, and a sustained focus on the terrorist threat.

Research source
03

Brian Clark

Financial Services • Euro Brokers

Brian Clark was a senior executive at Euro Brokers who survived the collapse of the South Tower after helping others find a path to safety. His professional career and actions on September 11 reflect both business leadership and personal courage.

Ron DiFrancesco

Financial Services • South Tower Survivor

Ron DiFrancesco worked at Fuji Bank in the South Tower and became one of the final people to escape the building after the impact. His experience represents both a professional life in financial services and an extraordinary survival story.

Purpose

Our Mission

Legacy Honor 911 exists to remember people not only by the circumstances of their deaths, but also by the professional lives they built before September 11, 2001.

The World Trade Center represented a remarkable concentration of business, finance, entrepreneurship, leadership, technology, international commerce, and professional ambition. The people who worked there contributed to that history through the organizations they led, businesses they built, careers they developed, and work they pursued.

This project seeks to preserve those documented professional legacies in a respectful and accessible format so that future generations can learn more about the individuals behind the names.

The project is intentionally independent. It does not claim to speak for families, estates, employers, institutions, government agencies, survivors, or memorial organizations.

Our Principles

  • Respect. Remember individuals as people with complete lives and identities.
  • Accuracy. Prefer documented information and credible sources.
  • Transparency. Clearly distinguish historical research from the separate $LHR911 digital community project.
  • Independence. Do not imply authorization, endorsement, sponsorship, or representation without evidence.
  • Preservation. Keep professional accomplishments and life stories accessible for future generations.
Transparency

About Legacy Honor 911

Legacy Honor 911 is an independently created educational and memorial project. The project researches publicly available information about people associated with the World Trade Center on September 11, 2001, with particular attention to their professional careers, accomplishments, leadership, entrepreneurship, and the organizations and work they contributed to during their lives.

The project is not intended to replace established memorials, historical archives, government records, family histories, or institutional collections. Instead, it is intended to provide another accessible place where professional histories can be remembered and researched.

Important distinction: Legacy Honor 911 is independent and does not claim to represent, speak for, or act on behalf of any featured individual, family, estate, survivor, employer, institution, government agency, or memorial organization unless that relationship is expressly established and identified.
