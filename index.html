Purpose

Our Mission

Legacy Honor 911 exists to remember people through more than the circumstances of their deaths.

The project seeks to bring documented professional accomplishments into the light of remembrance: the careers people built, the organizations they served, the leadership they demonstrated, and the work they contributed to their communities and professions.

The intention is educational, historical, and memorial. The people presented here should be understood as individuals with lives, careers, relationships, ambitions, and accomplishments—not merely as names associated with a tragedy.

This project is independently created and is not an official memorial, governmental project, employer publication, or family archive.

Our Principles

  • Remember people as individuals.
  • Focus on documented professional legacies.
  • Use public information responsibly.
  • Correct information when reliable sources warrant it.
  • Do not rank the value of human lives.
  • Do not claim to speak for families or estates.
  • Respect intellectual-property and privacy boundaries.
