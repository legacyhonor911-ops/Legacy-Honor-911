Our Mission

Legacy Honor 911 is an independent educational, historical, and memorial project created to encourage people to learn about the professional lives and accomplishments of individuals connected with the World Trade Center on September 11, 2001.

The project emphasizes careers, leadership, entrepreneurship, professional achievement, and organizational contributions. The purpose is not to rank the value of anyone's life or suggest that one person's accomplishments were more important than another's.

Information presented on this website is based on public research and reasonable efforts to identify reliable sources. Corrections and additional reliable historical information are welcome.
