# Legacy Honor 911

**Remember. Honor. Preserve.**

Legacy Honor 911 is an independent educational and memorial project focused on preserving documented professional legacies associated with the World Trade Center on September 11, 2001.

## Purpose

The project remembers people through the professional lives, work, leadership, organizations, ambitions, and contributions that formed part of their documented legacies. It does not rank the value of lives or suggest that professional achievement determines human worth.

## Independence

Legacy Honor 911 is independently created and operated. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or representative of any family, estate, employer, financial institution, memorial organization, government agency, or other third party unless explicitly stated and documented.

## Research and corrections

Biographical and professional information is presented from publicly available sources and is intended for educational and memorial purposes. Sources should be reviewed carefully, and reasonable corrections or additional documentation are welcomed through the project's official contact channel.
